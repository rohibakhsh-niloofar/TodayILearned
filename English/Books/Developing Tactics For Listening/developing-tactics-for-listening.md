## Developing Tactics For Listening


### Unit 1 (The weekend)

|No.|Words|Definitions or Examples|
|--|--|--|
|1.|Weekend|Thursday and Friday|
|2.|Date|1. Meeting sb / 2. Exact time of sth / 3. A kind of sweet dessert|
|3.|nightclub|A place where people go to dance and drink, which is open late at night|
|4.|Make (sb) dinner |cook for someone|
|5.|How come?|Why? / How something happen? |
|6.|Competition|Contest|
|7.|Gee|To show your surprise|
|8.|Muscles|Part of your body|
|9.|Nephew|Sons of your siblings|
|10.|Exhausted|Too tired, Worn out, Done in (sth)|
|11.|Good-looking|handsome or pretty `it can use for both man and female`|
|12.|Science Fiction|Imaginary people or events in the future|
|13.|Wet (Weather)|Rainy|
|14.|Yuck|To show something is very unpleased / `Eww`|
|15.|Over the weekend|during the weekend|
|16.|Outdoor concert|the type of concert that stayed in out|
|17.|Pull out|withdraw|
|18.|Prize|gift, present|
|19.|A weekend for two|One weekend for two person|
|20.|Closest friend|Best Friend|
|21.|Pleasant|delightful|
|22.|Nature Walk|Hiking|
|23.|Near|≠ far|
|24.|Supposed to |Used to say that something is expected, required, or plan to happen. `I supposed to study`|
|25.|Wild| ≠ tame|
|26.|Along somewhere|through somewhere|
|27.|Trail|Path|
|28.|Sort of|Kind of|
|29.|Not much|nothing important|
|30.|Court|Where we play tennis|
|31.|Crowded|full of people|
